## Coverage summary

- **Official source records:** 59 non-article pages and 54 blog articles (113 source URLs in total). The stored page/article URL inventory was compared with the official sitemap and matched all 113 canonical URLs.
- **Local production routes:** the generated sitemap contains 119 routes. Every route returned HTTP 200 in the production-build browser check.
- **Official source text:** 955 page content blocks and 2399 full blog-body blocks across 54 articles; no article has an empty body.
- **Original image coverage:** 509 local image references across page OG/gallery images and blog covers/galleries, 368 unique files, 0 missing files.
- **Catalogue:** 9 categories, 48 individual product routes; all category cards were browser-checked. There are 47 distinct product-card image files for 48 cards because the two erosion-blanket variants use the same official blanket image.
- **No-backend behavior:** the contact enquiry form opens a prefilled email composer; quote/sample buttons link to Contact; direct email, phone and WhatsApp links use the supplied official details.
